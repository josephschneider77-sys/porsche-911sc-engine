# Catalogue references (Kat 502)

Source of truth for part number, quantity, shape, proportions, features, placement and orientation: the official Porsche parts catalogue, USA 911 1983, Kat 502 (`kat502-usa-911-83-katalog.pdf`). The PDF is not in the repo. Page numbers below are the PDF page index (the sheet itself is numbered as sheet “- 1” of that illustration). Photos are for finish and colour only.

The parts on this page are cited for the 1978 US engine, type **930/04**. Kat 502 summary engines (PDF p.7) lists `911 SC (USA)(CDN)`, engine type **930.04**, model year 78, engine number `628 0001>>`. California the same year is 930.06; the RoW 911 SC is 930.03. Each illustration below is headed `Model: 911 78`, `Model life 1978>>1983`. A blank model column applies to every model on that illustration, so the line covers 1978 USA 930/04. A model column of `SC` covers it for the same reason: 930.04 is the 1978 USA/CDN 911 SC. A year remark restricts the line: `78` is 1978, `-79` is through 1979, `80-` is 1980 on.

Plugs enter through the upper lid. Illustration 103-05 (drawing PDF p.66, list p.67–68) draws lid position 17, 901 105 115 03, with two round machined holes about one cylinder pitch apart and a half-round opening at one end: three plug openings per bank. Each has a cast collar on the underside, between the rocker pockets and outside the oil space. The upper lid outline is a long rounded-end pan; each long edge has three studs (six per cover, twelve per engine). Gasket position 18, 930 105 194 00, has separate windows and bridges where those collars sit, and six stud holes. Lid position 19, 930 105 116 00, is the exhaust-side pan (PDF p.66): no plug holes, rounded ends, diagonal ribs, and three raised lugs between the studs on one long edge. The pan takes eleven hex studs per cover (twenty-two per engine, position 15) plus the three special cap nuts on those lugs (position 24, 901 111 271 00). Gasket position 20, 930 105 195 01, is one closed ring with webs between the openings, following that flange. Hex nuts are position 25, 900 076 025 02, thirty-four per engine (six on each upper lid, eleven on each lower lid). Each nut and each cap nut carries its washer and points out along the cover normal, seated on the stud axis. Illustration 901-00 (drawing PDF p.582, list p.583–584) draws the plug (position 16) and the connector (position 21, 911 602 315 00) as a long straight tube, a round seal flange near the outer end, and a 90° elbow.

The bore is coaxial with the cover hole. The upper-cover normal on the right bank is (0.742, 0.671, 0), 42.1° above horizontal. The datum is head-local (2.36, −20, 34): the centre electrode is 0.75 mm behind it and the ground strap’s outer face is 0.75 mm in front, 2.3 mm clear of the piston crown at TDC. The mesh gap is 0.8 mm. The hole is 51 mm from the cylinder centre along the row and 8 mm toward the cam side of the lid (rearward on cylinders 1–3, forward on 4–6). The axis from that datum to the hole is (0.8009, 0.5871, 0.1181): 35.95° above horizontal, 36.79° off the cylinder axis, 8.98° off the cover normal. The cover seat moved +17 mm outboard, so the path from the tip to the connector’s seal flange (`SPARK_FLANGE_T`) is 156.7 mm. Plug poses (tip, axis, roll) are unchanged. Cylinder 6’s station falls past the flywheel end of the cover rail, because the rail is centred on the cam housing and the left bank is staggered toward the flywheel. That opening is a half-round scallop in the end of the upper-left lid, on the same bore. The seal rail is not lengthened. The exhaust flange is the port plate only. The head washer seat is the spot-face at the end of the 19 mm reach, and the M14 minor-diameter bore runs from the chamber out through the casting. The connector tube clears the hole; only its seal flange seats there.

Dimensions used with the catalogue, and not printed on these sheets:

- Head height, sealing face to cam-housing face: 84.48 mm (Klassik ATS). The model is still `HEAD_OUT_X − CYL_TOP_X` = 61 mm, about 23.5 mm short, and that casting is not lengthened here.
- Valve angles: 25.5° intake / 30.25° exhaust, 55.75° included (Pelican forum 851122).
- Valve lengths, face to tip: 110.1 mm intake / 108.4 mm exhaust (Design911 and KS 3051).
- Cam: FVD 930 105 147 17 / Cat Cams. Base radius 14.7 mm, lobe lift 7.75 mm intake / 6.90 mm exhaust. Valve lift with 0.10 mm lash is 11.3 mm intake / 9.9 mm exhaust.
- Rocker ratio: Pelican forum 1054198. The slipper is Kat 502 103-10 position 48. Measured ratio on this layout is about 1.47 average, about 1.64 / 1.58 at the nose.

Position 45 is a pan-head screw. The model uses that head, not a hex socket. Six screws and six nuts on each bank. The nut’s flange sits on the housing spot face; the cone enters the shaft.

Kat 502 lists one rocker forging, **930 105 043 00**, quantity 12, so intake and exhaust are the same part. One outline does not fit both stations on this head. The slipper radii are 36 mm intake and 30 mm exhaust, the eye arms are 34.7 mm and 39.8 mm, and the shafts sit at different places (intake 314, +35 and exhaust 308, −38, cam still at 292). A single shape cannot put both balls on the stem at the two lifts (11.29 mm intake, 9.90 mm exhaust) with the pad on the base circle. The model therefore keeps two machined outlines of that one forging number.

Every rocker-gear line above (positions 44–50, PDF p.73) has a blank remark and a blank model column. The illustration’s model life is 1978>>1983, so these lines cover 1978 USA 930/04. 930 105 043 02 is the other rocker-arm line, and 901 105 344 03 is the other bush. The quantity is 12 for the engine; illustration 103-15 positions 44 and 45 are circlips, not a second set of rockers.

## Air injection — illustration 108-00 (head tube not modelled)

The 1978 US heads take air-injection fittings in the catalogue. Illustration 108-00 (PDF p.142–143) is titled “Air injection”, model column `911 SC`, model life 1978>>1983. Sheet 103-00 does not list the port as its own part. The fittings are position 18, unions **911 113 145 02** and **911 113 145 04**, qty 6, and position 19, sealing ring **900 123 033 20**, qty 6, A 10×13.5 CU. Those three rows have a blank remark and a blank model column, so they apply to the 911 SC illustration, which includes 1978 USA 930/04. The head air tube (108-00 #18–#20) is not modelled here. The pump, hoses, heater blower and EGR are in the model from the bottom-end work on main. Illustration 108-05 is the Turbo air injection and is not this engine.

# Catalogue references for the 1978 US (930/04) intake and fuel

Shapes, part numbers and quantities for the intake and fuel system follow Porsche catalogue **Kat 502 USA 911 '83** (the PDF is not stored in this repo). Photos are used only for finish and colour. Where a page gives no millimetre dimension, the model scales the drawing and marks the result **E** in `docs/engine-spec.md`.

This model is engine type **930/04** (1978 US, not California). It is not 930/03 (RoW) and not the California engine. Engine-type pages: PDF pp. 4 and 7.
