# Catalogue references for the 1978 US (930/04) intake and fuel

Shapes, part numbers and quantities for the intake and fuel system follow Porsche catalogue **Kat 502 USA 911 '83** (the PDF is not stored in this repo). Photos are used only for finish and colour. Where a page gives no millimetre dimension, the model scales the drawing and marks the result **E** in `docs/engine-spec.md`.

This model is engine type **930/04** (1978 US, not California). It is not 930/03 (RoW) and not the California engine. Engine-type pages: PDF pp. 4 and 7.

| Illustration | PDF page | What this model takes from it |
|---|---|---|
| 106-00 | 97 | Air distributor and intake pipes. Housing **911 110 106 13** (#9, 49-state, not the California 911 110 106 15). Cylinder 6 pipe **911 110 490 06** (#6, not the California/Japan 911 110 420 06). |
| 107-00 | 106 | Mixture control unit. Fuel distributor **911 110 967 00** (#1) is the square block with outlet towers in a ring. Air-flow meter **911 110 965 00** (#2). The A 8×11.5 ring on this page is the scale for the outlet circle (Ø76 mm, E). |
| 107-10 | 110 (drawing), 111–113 (text) | Fuel circuit, throttle, air guide, auxiliary air. US regulator **911 606 102 04** (#36, remark USA), not RoW 911 606 102 01. Throttle housing **930 110 248 02** (#4) with O-ring 67.5×4 (#6). Air guide **930 110 358 05** (#18), clamps S 131/9 (#19) and S 85/9 (#20). Injector lines **911 110 093 11** / **12** (#23). Sealing rings A 8×11.5 (#24, #60). |

Group 107-15 (1980–83) is not used. The breather hose 901 107 394 00 and the heater hoses are left off.
