# Catalogue citations (Kat 502, USA 911 1978–83)

Source of truth: Porsche parts catalogue Kat 502. Page numbers are the PDF page labels printed on the sheets (Bild 103-05 on p.66, Bild 103-10 on p.70, Bild 103-15 on p.74). Photos decide finish and colour only.

This model is engine type **930/04** (1978 US, not California). It is not 930/03 (RoW) and not the California engine. Engine-type pages: PDF pp. 4 and 7.

## Intake and fuel

Shapes, part numbers and quantities for the intake and fuel system follow Porsche catalogue **Kat 502 USA 911 '83** (the PDF is not stored in this repo). Photos are used only for finish and colour. Where a page gives no millimetre dimension, the model scales the drawing and marks the result **E** in `docs/engine-spec.md`.

|---|---|---|
| 106-00 | 97 | Air distributor and intake pipes. Housing **911 110 106 13** (#9, 49-state, not the California 911 110 106 15) is the tray, with the snout on lid **930 110 184 00** (#14). Six pipes **911 110 420 06 / 470 06 / 480 06 / 440 06 / 450 06 / 490 06** (#1–#6). Cylinder 6 is **911 110 490 06**, not the California/Japan 911 110 420 06. Sleeves **928 110 158 01** (#10). Struts **911 110 133 02** (#18) and **911 110 269 00** (#19, tags -80), buffers **911 110 154 00** (#20). Pipe 3 is drawn with two studs; the text extract has no 3A line. |
| 107-00 | 106 | Mixture control unit. | 107-10 | 110 (drawing), 111–113 (text) | Fuel circuit, throttle, air guide, auxiliary air, vacuum. US regulator **911 606 102 04** (#36, remark USA), not RoW 911 606 102 01. Additional air valve **911 110 273 00** (#39, tags -80) with support **911 110 274 01** (#40). Throttle housing **930 110 248 02** (#4) with O-ring 67.5×4 (#6). The diverter hose 108-00 #31 does not take a fourth port on that tee. Cold-start intermediate piece **911 110 264 00** (#32). Fuel line **930 110 502 00** (#51) leaves screw socket #49 on the distributor hub (union nut) for the warm-up connection piece #52. Group 107-15 (1980–83) is not used. The breather hose 901 107 394 00 and the heater hoses are left off. Illustrations 108-00 (air injection) and 202-05 (EGR) are not in the checklist extract. Page 141 (108-00) draws hose **999 239 003 40** #31 (diverter valve, 3.2×7, 750 mm) as an L leaving the diverter's upper nipple. The other end runs off the drawing, and no T-piece is listed. Page 177 (202-05) draws two small nipples side by side at the upper left, on the intake/throttle side, parallel and about 11 mm apart out of the same boss. EGR hose #15 (40 mm) runs from one of them to T-piece #18. That nipple is `THROTTLE_PORTED_VAC`. The second nipple is `TEE_AIR_INJ`, cast into the throttle housing. Both point straight down. The far end of #31 is not drawn; the seat is inferred from that pair. 202-05 #18 is **999 137 004 40**, quantity 1, and Bottom End models it as `egr-tee`. The manifold splice is gone. The hose from thermo valve 17A to 107-10 #14 is cut **#13, 245 mm** (N 020 353 5, 3.5×2). #13A is 30 mm, #13B is 370 mm and #16 is 40 mm of 8×14. 202-05 #15 (40 mm, throttle nipple to T #18), #16 (770 mm) and #17 (two cuts of 465 mm) are not part of the nine-piece 107-10 harness. `docs/photo-refs/sheet-vacuum-harness.png` is the hose, barb and clamp close-up: the bore sits on the barb, and the wire sits on the hose over that overlap. `docs/photo-refs/sheet-paired-nipples.png` puts that p.177 reading beside a render of the removed splice and a render of the cast pair. The catalogue host would not serve the page image, so the drawing column quotes the crop instead of pasting it.
